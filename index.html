Paperwise Privacy Policy

Effective: November 4, 2026 · App: Paperwise for iPhone

In short: Paperwise does not collect, store or share any of your personal data. Your scans stay on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Everything you scan or import, the text Paperwise recognizes in it, and any passwords you set on PDFs are processed and stored only on your iPhone, inside the app's own storage (visible to you in the Files app). Paperwise has no servers and no user accounts, so we never receive your documents.

Camera and photos

Paperwise uses the camera only while you are scanning, and reads photos only when you pick them. Saving pages to Photos happens only when you ask for it. You can change these permissions any time in Settings › Paperwise.

Reminders and Calendar

When you tap "Remind Me", Paperwise adds a reminder to Apple Reminders or opens Apple's own add-event screen for Calendar. This happens only when you ask, and only for the date you chose. Paperwise asks for Reminders access the first time and never reads your existing reminders or events.

Text recognition and sorting

Reading the text in your scans, detecting document types and names, and finding dates all happen on your iPhone using Apple's built-in technology. Nothing is sent anywhere for this.

Sharing

When you share, print or save a scan, it goes only to the app, printer or location you choose, through Apple's standard share sheet. What happens to it there is governed by that app's or service's own policy.

No tracking, analytics or ads

Paperwise contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking code, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

The optional Paperwise Pro upgrade is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we receive no payment card or personal details, only Apple's confirmation that the upgrade is unlocked.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or computer backups, your iPhone may include Paperwise's files in them, under Apple's terms. Paperwise itself does not upload anything.

Children

Paperwise is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
